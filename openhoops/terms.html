Reporting and blocking

Every player has two tools, available from another player's profile in the app:

  1. Block. Blocking hides you and the other player from each other on court rosters and player profiles, and stops thumbs-up between you.
  2. Report. Reporting sends us the player, a category, and where it happened. Reports are private — the reported player is never told who reported them. A report is available to us for review, and we may warn, suspend, or remove an account.

We are a small operation and can't promise that a report will be read, or a response time, an outcome, or an explanation of what we decided. You can also write to support@timbuilds.dev, which is the right route if the person you want to report is no longer visible in the app.

Check-ins and location are not guarantees

Everything OpenHoops shows about who is where comes from other players' devices, and it can be wrong.

When you check in, the app compares your device's location to the venue's and only allows the check-in if you appear to be close enough. That is a rough check, not proof. Phone GPS drifts, especially indoors and in cities; venue coordinates in our directory may be inaccurate or out of date; a court may be closed, full, reserved, locked, or no longer exist; a person shown as "here" may have left without checking out; a "heading there" plan is an intention, not a commitment; and someone may simply be lying.

Treat rosters, counts, arrival times, and plans as hints, not facts. We don't verify that any player is actually at any venue, and we are not responsible for a wasted trip, a missed run, or anything else that follows from information in the app being wrong.
